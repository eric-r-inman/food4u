Longevity Pantry

"Not today, preventable diseases!"

Longevity Pantry is a foods-first kitchen companion, tracking longevity foods in your pantry and fridge, and using those foods to build recipes and shopping lists that support Mediterranean, Blue Zone, MIND, anti-inflammatory, and DASH approaches to healthful eating. Features require sign-in.

How it works

I developed Longevity Pantry because I want a super simple kitchen companion that makes it easy to know which science-backed longevity foods I have on hand, and which ones I need to shop for. I want to easily create recipes, meal plans, and shopping lists from those foods. Sign in to create your own kitchen, and let me know what you think! Please consider donating so I can continue to host and update the app.

  • Longevity Foods — More than 500 foods arranged by how often to eat them, based on science-backed, longevity-focused dietary guidelines. Add, move, or remove foods to make the list your own.
  • Recipes — Stocked with recipes inspired by longevity diets, organized by dish type, and filterable by tags. Curate the list to your liking, by adding or pasting in your own recipes.
  • Meal Planner — Plan your meals for the week by dragging recipe cards into breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snack slots.
  • Kitchen — Track longevity foods in your pantry, refrigerator, freezer, and spice cupboard. A staples tracker lets you keep tabs on the foods you always want to have on hand.
  • Shopping List — One-click add out-of-stock staples or ingredients for recipes. Or, drag-and-drop foods from the Longevity Foods list. When it's time to shop, export your shopping list as a text file.

Your data

When you're signed in, your kitchen is unique, and your changes are saved to the server. If you use the AI recipe assistant, you use your own key, and requests go straight from your browser to your chosen provider; the key never touches this server.
